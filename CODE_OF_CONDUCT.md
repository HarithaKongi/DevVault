# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

DevVault is intended to be a welcoming and constructive open-source project.

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Welcome different perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, not people.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct conflicts with these standards.

For serious concerns, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub.
